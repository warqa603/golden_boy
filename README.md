## 3. Local Data Storage on Device

All gameplay progression data is stored **locally on your device** via standard Android `SharedPreferences`. This includes:
- Completed stages and unlocked stadium worlds (105+ levels).
- Star coins and championship trophies collected.
- Daily stamina / attempts counter (30 hearts bank with 12-hour refill timer).
- Sound, music, and haptic vibration preferences.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud database. If you uninstall the game or clear the Application cache/storage through your device settings, your locally stored progression will be reset.

## 4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application displays advertisements powered by **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to support development and offer free gameplay features (such as voluntary rewarded video continues and optional extra lives).

Google AdMob may process non-sensitive device data and pseudonymous advertising identifiers to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. This data may include:
- **Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID):** A resettable device identifier used for advertising purposes.
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and mobile carrier/network type.
- **Coarse Geographic Data:** Country or region-level location derived from IP address (no precise GPS).
- **Ad Performance Metrics:** Impressions, click rates, and ad viewing duration.

### Ad Choices & User Controls
You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time directly through your Android device settings:
- Go to: **Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.

For comprehensive details on how Google processes advertising data, please review:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google Uses Information From Sites or Apps That Use Our Services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 5. Device Permissions Requested

The Application requests only the minimal permissions essential for gameplay and ad serving:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required solely to request and display advertisements via Google AdMob.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to verify internet connectivity before sending ad requests.
- `android.permission.VIBRATE`: Provides subtle physical haptic feedback during jumps, slide tackles, obstacle collisions, and goal celebrations. Can be toggled off at any time.
