This Privacy Policy explains how The Golden Boy ("Application", "Game", "we", "us", or "our"), published by Warqa Apps, handles data across Android mobile devices.

Our Application does not engage in background profiling or unnecessary data tracking. The Application:

3. Local Data Storage on Device

All gameplay progression is saved exclusively on your local Android device using standard Android SharedPreferences:

  • Completed stages and unlocked stadium worlds (105+ levels).
  • Collected star coins and championship trophies.
  • Stamina heart bank and recharge timers.
  • Audio volume and haptic vibration preferences.

This data never leaves your device. If you clear the application storage or uninstall the game, this local data will be permanently cleared from your device.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing game development and provide optional rewards (such as voluntary video continues), this Application displays advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Google AdMob may process non-personally identifiable device data and pseudonymous advertising identifiers, which may include:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): A user-resettable device identifier used for ads and fraud prevention.
  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and network connection type.
  • Coarse Location Data: Country or regional level derived from IP address (no GPS coordinates).
  • Ad Performance Metrics: Ad impressions, click activity, and view duration.

You can manage, reset, or delete your Advertising ID at any time via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads.

For more details on Google's privacy practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy

5. Application Permissions

The Application requests only the minimal necessary Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required strictly to load AdMob advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the Google Mobile Ads SDK to verify internet availability before making network calls.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback during jumps, tackles, and collisions.

The Application is designed for general audiences as a sports runner game. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will promptly address the inquiry.
